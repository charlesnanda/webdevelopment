Major Deities of Kemet

Ra / Atum
The sun god and creator, symbolizing light, warmth, and the daily cycle of renewal.
Osiris (Wesir)
The god of the afterlife, resurrection, and fertility.
Isis (Auset)
The goddess of magic, marriage, and motherhood.
Thoth
The god of wisdom, writing, and knowledge.
Hathor
The goddess of love, beauty, and joy.
